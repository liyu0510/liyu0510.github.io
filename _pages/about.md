Welcome!

I am an Assistant Professor at the [Li Anmin Institute of Economic Research](https://lier.lnu.edu.cn/) at Liaoning University.

My research is primarily in macroeconomics and macro-finance. I use macroeconometric methods and economic models to study the transmission of economic shocks through financial systems, with a particular interest in financial intermediation and the role of institutional structure.

A related strand of my research examines how market structure shapes price formation and cost pass-through, particularly in digital markets.

I also have broader interests in economic development and structural transformation, especially regional change in the Chinese economy.
